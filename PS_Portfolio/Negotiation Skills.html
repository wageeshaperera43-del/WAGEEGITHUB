    • BATNA, or Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement, refers to the option a person can pursue if negotiations fail to reach an agreement. Essentially, it’s a backup plan or Plan B.

    BATNA PROCESS

  • Step 1 : Identify your current situation.
  • Step 2 : Evaluate your BATNA.
  • Step 3 : Assess your current negotiation.
  • Step 4 : Determine your reservation point.
  • Step 5 : Use BATNA in the negotiation.
  • Step 6 : Negotiate based on BATNA.

    • ZOPA - Zone of Possible Agreement

    Strong negotiation abilities are important in many areas of life, such as in business, personal relationships, and resolving conflicts.It is the span in a negotiation where both sides can find a mutually acceptable deal. It is important for successful negotiations because it shows the area where an agreement that satisfies both parties is possible.

    ZOPA Process

  • Step 1 : Identify your Aspiration range.
  • Step 2 : Identify other party’s Aspiration range.
  • Step 3 : Determine the overlapping range.
  • Step 4 : Prepare and communicate effectively.
  • Step 5 : Engage in negotiation.
  • Step 6 : Reach an agreement.
