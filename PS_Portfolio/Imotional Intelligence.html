EMOTIONAL INTELLIGENCE

What is an Emotion?

An emotion is a natural reaction to an event, such as joy, anger, or fear, and it influences our thoughts, actions, and the way we express ourselves.

Primary and Secondary Emotions

Primary Emotion Secondary Emotions / Examples
Sadness Grief, sorrow, depression
Fear Anxiety, nervousness, wariness
Enjoyment Happiness, joy, contentment
Love Acceptance, trust, infatuation
Surprise Shock, astonishment, wonder
Disgust Contempt, scorn, aversion
Shame Guilt, embarrassment, remorse

    • What is Emotional Intelligence

    Emotional intelligence is the skill to identify, comprehend, and control your own emotions as well as your interactions with others. It aids in shaping behavior, interpreting social signals, and forming meaningful relationships.

    Key skills to work on to develop EI

  • Perceive emotions in oneself and others.
  • Understanding emotional meanings.
  • Use emotions to facilitate thinking.
  • Managing emotions.

    • EQ VS IQ

    EQ (Emotional Quotient)measures emotional intelligence—your ability to understand, manage, and use emotions to achieve goals and relate well to others.


    IQ (Intelligence Quotient)measures cognitive abilities like learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and understanding. It’s assessed through tasks like puzzles, memory, and analogies.

    Components of Emotional Intelligence

  • Self-awareness — Knowing what you are feeling.
  • Self-regulation — Mindfulness, acceptance, problem solving.
  • Motivation — Staying persistent through setbacks to reach important goals.
  • Empathy — Sensing what others feel.
  • Social Skills — Communication, influence, conflict management.

    • Emotional Intelligence and Leadership

    Leading Others:The ability to understand and respond to the personal feelings of others.

    Leading Self:Recognizing your emotions and acting on them in a reflective and rational manner.
