INTERVIEW SKILLS

A job interview is a professional conversation between an employer and a candidate, aimed at evaluating the candidate’s skills, experience, and overall suitability for the role. It also provides the candidate an opportunity to learn more about the organization, its culture, and expectations, making it a two-way process of assessment.

The Best Interview Strategy

The most effective interview strategy is the one you create by reflecting on yourself.

  • What do I want the interviewer to know about me?
  • What weaknesses should I minimize?
  • What do I need to learn about the job or organization?

    • THE role of effective communication

    In an interview, the way you present yourself through your behavior and responses reflects your personality, confidence, and attitude. Creating a strong impression depends on how effectively you communicate.

    The communication skills used at job interviews

  • Non-verbal communication
  • Listening
  • Responding
  • Questioning

    • Effective communication strategies Dduring a job interview

  • Know the job/company well.
  • Respond to topic shift.
  • Use industry terms and technical jargon.
  • Use specifics and examples.
  • Ask questions where appropriate.
  • Speak more than interviewer.

    • Successful VS Unsuccesful Candidates

    Successful Candidates Unsuccessful Candidates
    Were specific & consistent Had vague ideas
    Used company name Rarely used company name
    Showed research about company Didn’t know about the company
    Used technical jargon Didn’t use jargon
    Asked questions Asked few questions
    Spoke more than interviewer Gave short answers
    Made eye contact, smiled Made little eye contact
    Accepted topic shift Resisted topic shift

    Active Listening

    Being a good listener is one of the key skills you can develop.

  • Pay attention.
  • Show that you are listening.
  • Seek clarification if you don’t understand.
  • Deter judgment.
  • Respond appropriately.
