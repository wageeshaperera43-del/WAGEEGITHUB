FOOD FESTIVAL DAY

For our Professional Skills module assignment, the Year 2 Semester 2 team organized a Food Festival on 29th July at the BOC Ground Floor. The event was carried out through collective teamwork, with several groups managing stalls that offered a variety of foods while applying different strategies to promote and sell their products.

What did we sell?

  • Shrimp Wade
  • Curd & Treacle
  • Sandwiches
  • Pancakes
  • Roti with Lunu miris

    OPERATION ON THAT DAY

    All team members actively managed the stall from beginning to end, handling sales, managing cash, and engaging with customers. Throughout the food festival, we promoted our items by inviting passersby to visit our stall, which helped attract a steady stream of students and staff. We were also fortunate to have our stall located next to the lifts, which naturally drew the attention of people as they stepped out. In addition, the unique variety of food we offered was well-suited to the lunchtime crowd, further increasing interest and attracting more visitors to our stall.
