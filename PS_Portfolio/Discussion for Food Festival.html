DISCUSSION FOR FOOD FESTIVAL

In this session, we concentrated on the planning and preparation stage for the upcoming food festival. The discussion highlighted key skills required for effective event management, such as teamwork, clear communication, problem-solving, and adaptability in changing situations. By the end of the session, we decided to name our group “Flaver Hut.”

Key Points

  • Focused on the planning and preparation phase of the food festival.
  • Identified essential skills for event success: teamwork, communication, problem-solving, and adaptability.
  • Shared ideas on how to handle dynamic and unexpected situations during the event.
  • Emphasized the importance of collaboration and effective coordination among team members.
  • Finalized the group name as “Flaver Hut.”
