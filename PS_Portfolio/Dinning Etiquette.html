DINING ETIQUETTE

What is Dining Etiquette?

Dining etiquette is the practice of following proper manners during meals. It includes the correct use of utensils, polite table behavior, and respectful interaction with others, helping create a pleasant and courteous dining experience.

Napkin Etiquette

  • Place the napkin on your lap after the host does.
  • Fold it neatly and use clean surfaces.
  • Do not use as a bib or crumple it.

    • Bread Etiquette

  • Break bread into small pieces before eating.
  • Use a bread plate and take a small amount of butter.
  • Never bite directly into a whole slice or roll.

    • Salt & pepper Etiquette

  • Always pass salt and pepper together.
  • Never deny someone a condiment request.
  • Politeness and consistency matter at the table.

    • How to Drink soup

  • Dip the spoon away from you.
  • Sip quietly from the side of the spoon.
  • Tilt the bowl away, never blow to cool.

    • Proper Table Setting

  • Understand the layout: plates, glasses, and utensils.
  • Forks on the left, knives and spoons on the right.
  • Glasses go above the knives.

    • Red Wine Glass VS White Wine Glass

  • Red wine glass: larger bowl for aeration.
  • White wine glass: smaller to preserve cool temperature.
  • Know the difference for proper use.

    • Cutlery Placement Signals

  • Finished: Fork and knife together at 4 o’clock.
  • Pause: Knife and fork crossed.
  • Don’t place used cutlery back on the table.
